# DESIGN.md — Gardenia Night Gold (Wedding Invite / RTL)  ## Product A cinematic, premium, mobile-first wedding invitation website in Arabic (RTL). One-page experience with scroll and swipe storytelling.  ## Direction & Language - Primary language: Arabic - Layout direction: RTL - Tone: luxurious, modern, minimal; no cheesy templates; no hearts; no stocky wedding clipart.  ## Color System (Tokens) Use a dark “charcoal night” base with warm gold accents.  ### Core - Background / Night Charcoal: #0B0D10 - Surface / Deep Onyx: #11151B - Surface 2 / Card: #141A22 - Text / Primary: #F5F2EA - Text / Secondary: #CFC8BC - Muted / Divider: #2A3442  ### Accent (Gold) - Gold Primary: #D4AF37 - Gold Warm Highlight: #F0D277 - Gold Soft Glow (used sparingly): #FFDFA6  ### Feedback - Success: #3DDC97 - Warning: #F6C177 - Danger: #FF5A6A  ### Usage Rules - Gold is for: CTAs, highlights, active states, progress indicators. - Keep backgrounds dark and calm; avoid high-saturation gradients. - Maintain WCAG-friendly contrast for text on dark surfaces.  ## Typography Use elegant Arabic for headings + modern Arabic for body.  - Headings: "Amiri" (fallback: "Noto Naskh Arabic", serif) - Body/UI: "Cairo" (fallback: "Tajawal", sans-serif)  ### Type Scale (mobile-first) - Display: 42–52px (Hero names) - H1: 30–34px - H2: 22–26px - Body: 16–18px - Small: 13–14px - Micro: 11–12px  ### Styling - Letter spacing: very subtle; avoid wide tracking on Arabic - Headings: high contrast, spacious line-height  ## Layout & Spacing - Base spacing unit: 8px - Page padding: 18–22px mobile - Max content width: 560px on larger screens; center the column - Corner radius:   - Cards: 16px   - Buttons: 14px   - Pills/Chips: 999px (pill)  ## Components (Visual Rules) ### Buttons - Primary CTA: gold gradient subtle (Gold Primary -> Gold Warm Highlight) - Text on gold: #0B0D10 - States: default / pressed (slightly darker) / focus ring (soft gold glow) - Keep buttons large for thumb reach.  ### Cards (for Swipe Story) - Background: Surface 2 (#141A22) - Border: 1px solid rgba(212,175,55,0.18) - Active card: slight scale up + soft glow - Include a small top progress indicator option.  ### Countdown - Large numeric blocks with crisp alignment - Use gold separators or micro-lines - Background: subtle animated particles (very low intensity)  ### Accordion (FAQ) - Minimal lines, smooth open/close - Chevron icon in gold muted - Expanded state: slightly brighter border and softer shadow  ### Modal/Lightbox - Backdrop: rgba(11,13,16,0.72) - Modal surface: #11151B - Close button: top-left (RTL-friendly placement can be top-right), gold outline  ## Motion & Interaction (Premium) Prefer performant animations (transform/opacity). Avoid heavy blur/shadow animations.  ### Global Motion Tokens - Durations:   - Fast: 160–220ms   - Medium: 280–420ms   - Slow: 520–700ms - Easing:   - Use “easeOutCubic” feel for reveals   - Use “easeInOutCubic” feel for transitions  ### Scroll Reveal - Pattern: fade + translateY(12–22px) + very light blur (optional) - Use stagger in groups (title -> body -> buttons)  ### Parallax - Hero has 2–3 layers with subtle movement - Very slow and minimal; never distract from text  ### Swipe Story - Horizontal swipe cards with scroll-snap mandatory - Active/Prev/Next states should feel like “story carousel” - Include small progress dots or bar  ### Reduced Motion If prefers-reduced-motion: - Disable parallax - Reduce reveal motion (fade only) - Keep swipe without scale/glow exaggeration  ## Content Rules (Hard Constraints) - Do NOT mention parents/family names. - Do NOT include RSVP or attendance form. - Do NOT include “كتب كتاب”. - Do NOT include dress code. - Single venue (no men/women separate halls).  ## Imagery & Graphics - Use custom minimal SVG ornaments (Arabic-inspired lines, star dust particles) - No cliché wedding icons; no obvious templates; no random stock photos.
